# Prospective validation plan

This document describes work that would be required before physical or
regulated use. It does not claim that such validation has occurred.

## Installation and operational checks

- Verify robot, pipette, labware, and software versions.
- Confirm deck calibration and pipette calibration status.
- Verify labware definitions and physical placement.
- Challenge invalid parameters and insufficient consumables.

## Performance qualification

| Characteristic | Proposed test | Example acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Gravimetric measurement at 20, 100, and 180 uL | Pre-approved method-specific limit |
| Precision | At least 10 replicates per volume | Pre-approved CV limit |
| Dilution linearity | Quantified dye dilution series | Pre-approved regression limit |
| Carryover | High sample followed by blank | Below validated detection threshold |
| Plate identity | Reconcile manifest against output wells | 100% agreement |
| Recovery | Simulate tip shortage and interruption | Controlled stop and documented recovery |

Acceptance criteria must be approved by the responsible quality and scientific
functions before execution.

## Documentation

- Approved protocol version and change history
- Raw measurement files and calculation records
- Deviations, investigations, and corrective actions
- Final validation report and authorized release decision
